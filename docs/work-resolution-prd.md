# Resolve interrupted and waiting work

## User outcome

A person sees what blocks work and can continue from that location. They do not have to refresh an external status, interpret a technical recovery recommendation, find another page and then retry. Routine account-reading expiry is background observation, not a demand for attention.

## Behavior

- An explicit owner Stop or refusal ends the current request for attention. Stopped/completed goals and cancelled/completed executions belong to results/history, even when an earlier recovery record still says checking or needs input. Historical recovery, failure and external-cleanup evidence remains inspectable; it cannot create a current retry decision, active-work count or pending-work badge. Only a fresh owner continuation can make that work current again.
- Apply the same current-state precedence to global and project workbenches, selected goal/execution details, summary projections and acknowledged-receipt overlays. A later Stop or terminal observation supersedes an older recovery receipt. Old records do not require a private-data migration to render correctly. Genuine failed work, human/review/account waits and newly authorized recovery retain their actions.
- For a failed or held stage, show one primary Retry step / Continue step action beside the actual state, with an optional note in the ordinary surface. The note reaches the next attempt as feedback. A requested continuation checks current state and reconciles the exact external task automatically before retrying; it never repeats already accepted stages.
- Reconciliation has a technical deadline and never starts another execution while external termination is unconfirmed. If it cannot establish safety, retain the result and expose a concrete external-inspection/Check again action. Preserve permissions, scope and allowed models; deprecated numeric execution quotas do not constrain continuation.
- Goal coordinators use their decision-scoped termination evidence. A confirmed local preparation failure with no external assignment resumes coordination without searching for an unrelated pipeline-stage record. Confirmed terminal coordinator tasks follow the same rule. Unknown coordinator termination and unconfirmed delegated work still require reconciliation; retain the original attempt and recovery history.
- An explicit continuation of a stopped goal with failed delegated work resumes that exact step before another coordinator decision. It retains completed stages, their artifacts, the execution checkout and frozen pipeline/knowledge. The owner selects the run, stage and previous attempt with revision checks; this does not weaken the general closed-goal guard or replay the pipeline.
- The owner may explicitly use the current same-role browser configuration for that step's next attempt. Pin the project revision, exact origins, prior attempt, owner and note in a durable continuation record. Apply only `browser.use` and its binding; retain the frozen sandbox, model, instructions and other tools. Historical snapshots and attempts retain their original configuration. Default and automatic retries do not acquire this capability. Reject stale project/attempt/control revisions and unconfirmed cleanup before dispatch. The new attempt shows the applied browser authorization separately from its original frozen basis.
- The same explicit browser choice is available when a goal is failed and when a prior cleanup check needs another observation. If today's same-role browser differs from the failed attempt, the goal's retry/check action opens the exact-step continuation form rather than silently retrying the frozen browser binding. Keep the optional recovery note when moving into this form. Identical browser authority keeps the ordinary direct continuation; every grant remains unchecked until deliberately selected.
- Compare the next attempt's authority: an already executed grant is consumed, while an unconsumed/admission-held grant can still be used. A dialog pins its opened goal/run/attempt controls; changed execution identity invalidates it. Bind a checked browser selection to the displayed project revision and origins, clearing that selection on configuration refresh. Opening/cancelling a dialog is not a write receipt and must retain the source draft.
- A failed-step continuation is model-free until the selected step starts. It deduplicates repeated requests across refresh/restart, obeys Stop during cleanup/account checks and retains real account approvals. Once QA succeeds, the existing scheduler proceeds to its queued downstream maintenance stage under that stage's original contract and review gate. Coordination resumes from actual retained results after delegated work ends.
- Historical coordinator cleanup uses its own project/run execution identity. A preparation failure may have a confirmed `not_dispatched` record without an accepted decision round; later rounds must not change that record's stage identity or make recovery repeatedly inspect a nonexistent decision. Both confirmation and explicit reconciliation use the same unambiguous recorded identity. Missing, conflicting or unconfirmed evidence remains a blocker; do not infer termination from an idle daemon or rewrite historical records.
- A genuine account uncertainty/near-limit decision shows its actual evidence and the corresponding explicit one-step action. Known exhaustion offers refresh and the real reset time. A healthy updated account reading does not require approval, and missing comparison samples do not require quota configuration. There is no execution-quota extension gate.
- Paid dispatch uses one freshly assessed observation under the admission lock and records that same service-issued basis. A second unconditional read must not create a false changed-reading hold. Preserve exact owner permits, authoritative exhaustion and fresh Stop/attempt checks.
- A continuation returns either a durable accepted run receipt or an explicit current decision without starting work. Refresh failure never converts a successful continuation into failure or resubmits it. A slow/failed decision read offers a retry-read and retains existing actions; it cannot spin indefinitely.
- Use the same actions from project/global goal inspection and pipeline stage inspection. Manual result/review waits keep their existing result and accept/revise controls; dependencies link to the named work; pause offers Resume/Stop. Do not display a text field where the executor cannot consume it.
- Quietly refresh account observations before they expire and again at submission. Routine expiry does not change a healthy summary into a warning or force a manual refresh. Failed refresh is shown at the point it actually prevents work, with details available on demand. Never claim an old reading is freshly confirmed.

## Acceptance

Cover changing healthy readings, caution/exhaustion/unknown data, forged or stale approvals, Stop during admission, single-click exact-scope reconciliation and continuation, retained accepted output, finite unknown cleanup, changed decision/limits, duplicate clicks, and successful continuation followed by failed observation. Include ordinary goal retry after a confirmed preparation failure or a terminal external coordinator task, plus unknown coordinator termination and unconfirmed delegated work. Add stopped/completed goals with every historical recovery phase, cancelled child runs with old manual/review/account gates, stale continuation receipts after Stop, and a fresh explicitly resumed recovery. Inspect global/project workbench counts, result badges and selected details in both locales. No model calls are needed for these state tests.

Include a stopped goal whose failed QA lacks browser permission in its frozen basis while the current role has a valid browser binding. Explicit owner continuation starts only the next QA attempt with that pinned binding, retains accepted upstream output and automatically reaches queued maintenance after QA succeeds. Verify the default retry stays on its old permissions, historical attempt configuration remains readable, stale/foreign targets and project changes fail closed, duplicate receipts survive restart, and Stop wins over delayed cleanup/account observations.
